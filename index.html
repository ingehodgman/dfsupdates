What's Happening? 🙋
WHAT HAPPENED IN MAY 📸

Rustig Trail was one to remember! 💚 A planned 9km somehow became 13km after a few wrong turns 😂 — but the extra distance just added to the memories. 15 ladies, one trail, one incredible team. We took on steep climbs, endless ladders, rock scrambling, itchy trees and a nerve-testing bridge — and got each other through every step. Tired, dusty and scratched by the end… but stronger, prouder, and so grateful for the sisterhood. Nobody gets left behind. ❤️🥾✨
