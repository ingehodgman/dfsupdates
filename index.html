Community Hours 🤝

Scholars looking to earn community hours — your giving contributes an enormous amount to our little home. Please visit the community hours web page and book your hours through this NPO, who then directs students to our little home.


Activities include: health & nutrition programs, art lessons, music & dance, exercise sessions, and sorting of received donations.


Note: We are not a baby home.
