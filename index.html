🥾🌿 Going Places — De Wildt Adventure Trails
Sometimes the smallest groups make the best memories (although we definitely missed a few faces)! This month there were just three of us tackling the 9.5km Peglerae Trail — and it turned out to be such a special morning. We were joined by our furry hiking buddy, Zoe, who happily explored every step of the trail with us! 🐾 The route was quite rocky, keeping us on our toes, but we were rewarded with beautiful trees, peaceful surroundings, fresh air and plenty of laughs along the way. A reminder that hiking isn’t about the size of the group — it’s about the journey, the nature and the wonderful company. Here’s to many more adventures, one step at a time. 💚

✨ Meet Deshni

Get to know her below 👇

1How do you balance your energy from a corporate environment to the dance floor?▾

Honestly, Zumba is how I reset. My corporate world is structured, strategic, and high-pressure — the dance floor is where I switch off the noise and just move. It’s less about balancing and more about transforming my energy from focused to free.

2What’s more intimidating — Monday boardroom or introducing a new Zumba routine?▾

Surprisingly… introducing a new routine! In the boardroom I know my content. On the dance floor, I need to win people over in seconds — if they’re not feeling the vibe, you know immediately 😄

3🔥 What’s your secret to keeping your boss energy AND dance floor charisma?▾

Authenticity. Whether I’m in a boardroom or on the dance floor, I show up as myself. The difference is the delivery — one is more structured, the other is more expressive — but the confidence comes from the same place.

4😅 Who sweats more — your staff or your Zumba students?▾

Definitely the Zumba students! My team sweats under deadlines… my Zumba divas sweat because I push them 😄

5💡 How do you turn stress into motivation?▾

I move. Whether it’s dancing, training, or just getting out of the mental space — movement clears my mind and resets my focus. By the time I’m done, stress turns into clarity.

6🏡 What does your family think about your Zumba moves?▾

They’ve accepted that I will randomly start dancing anywhere — kitchen, lounge, wherever 😄 They’re my biggest supporters… even if they laugh at me sometimes!

7👀 What’s the one thing people learn quickly about you?▾

That I bring energy into every room — whether it’s a client meeting or a Zumba class. I don’t do anything halfway.

8💛 Why Zumba? If not Zumba, what else would you teach?▾

Zumba is powerful because it doesn’t feel like exercise — it feels like joy. That’s what I love. If not Zumba, I’d probably do something else that blends movement and energy — maybe strength training or even something like dance fitness bootcamps.

1How do you balance your energy from a corporate environment to the dance floor?▾

Corporate has taught me discipline, Zumba taught me to release. Coffee fuels the corporate me throughout the day, music releases the real me.

2What’s more intimidating — your Monday morning boardroom presentation or introducing a new routine/song to your Zumba class?▾

Boardroom, 100%. The boardroom doesn’t dance back if you mess up. My Zumba class will hype me up even if I forget the steps 😄 And if I’m nervous, I make it a move and call it a choreo.

3What’s your secret to keeping your boss energy to dance floor charisma?▾

Desh has drilled this into me for 11 years and continues to challenge me LOL. I am learning that confidence is a cue, not a feeling. At work I lead teams, on the floor I lead movement — and I have learned it is the same skill, and that it makes people feel safe enough to go all in.

4Who sweats more around you — your staff at work or new Zumba students?▾

Zumba students, no contest. I’m nicer with my work teams than I am with the divas on the dance floor 😂

5How do you turn stress into motivation?▾

I move, literally. Yoga, dance, anything. Stress sits in your hips and can cripple you, so I reframe it — it just means my body cares about this. Then I channel whatever it is into movement. Trust me, I always feel better after movement.

6What does your family think about your Zumba moves?▾

They roast me at home and hype me up outside 😂 They think I’m showing off and I tell them it’s research. Haha! But secretly they’ve all tried some of my moves at family events — overall very supportive.

7What’s the one thing that people learn quickly about you?▾

That I show up with intention. Whether I’m running a project or running a class, people feel seen. And that I notice people — if someone’s ‘absent’ in class or at work, it matters to me.

8Why Zumba? If you didn’t teach Zumba, what else would you teach?▾

Zumba because after 11 years on the floor, I know what it feels like to find yourself in movement. I want to give people that for 60 mins, 2x a week. Joy is medicine. If not Zumba, probably yoga + kindness — I’m terrible at sitting still anyway; we’d probably be rolling down on our tummies if nothing else!

“By day I drive growth. By night I drive beats. Either way — I bring the energy.”
